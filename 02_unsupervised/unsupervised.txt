# Unsupervised Learning — What is it?

**Unsupervised Learning** is a type of Machine Learning where a model learns from **data without labels**.

### Simple example

Suppose we have customer data:

| Customer | Age | Income |
| -------- | --: | -----: |
| A        |  22 |    20k |
| B        |  25 |    25k |
| C        |  24 |    22k |
| D        |  45 |    80k |
| E        |  48 |    90k |

Here, we **don't tell the model**:

> "A, B, C are Group 1 and D, E are Group 2."

The model looks at the data itself and tries to find **patterns or groups**.

It may discover:

```text
        Customer Data
              ↓
       Unsupervised ML
              ↓
     ┌────────┴────────┐
     ↓                 ↓
Young/Lower         Older/Higher
Income Group        Income Group
```

### The key difference

**Supervised Learning:**

```text
Data + Labels
     ↓
   Model
     ↓
 Prediction
```

**Unsupervised Learning:**

```text
Data only
   ↓
 Model
   ↓
Find hidden patterns
```

### In one sentence

> **Unsupervised Learning teaches a machine to find hidden patterns, structures, or groups in data when we don't provide the answers (labels).**

The two major things we'll study are **Clustering** and **Dimensionality Reduction**.
